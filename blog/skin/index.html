Skin Conditions Treated by a Homeopathic Skin Doctor in Lucknow

Skin problems often have more than one trigger, including stress, hormones, digestion, diet and family history. Below are the conditions covered on this page, with signs to watch for and when to see a doctor.

Acne and Pimples

Acne shows up as pimples, blackheads, whiteheads or painful cysts, usually on the face, chest and back. Hormonal changes, stress, oily skin and conditions such as PCOD can play a part. If your acne comes with irregular periods, read our guide on PCOS and PCOD. See a doctor if acne is painful, leaves scars or has not improved with over-the-counter care.

Fungal Infections

Fungal infections such as ringworm cause itchy, round, red or scaly patches, often in skin folds, the groin, feet or scalp. They are more common in humid weather and can spread through shared towels or clothing. Get the rash checked if it keeps returning, spreads or does not respond to creams.

Psoriasis

Psoriasis causes thick, red patches covered with silvery scales, commonly on the elbows, knees and scalp, and it can flare and settle over time. It is a long-term condition, and homeopathic care here is meant to support overall management. Do not stop prescribed medicines without talking to your doctor. Seek care if joints become painful or swollen, or if patches spread widely.

Warts

Warts are small, rough growths caused by a virus, often on the hands, feet or face. Some go away on their own, while others stay for months or return. See a doctor if a growth bleeds, changes colour, grows quickly or is painful, because not every skin growth is a wart.

Pigmentation

Pigmentation includes dark patches, melasma, tanning and marks left behind by acne. Sun exposure, hormonal changes, skin injury and some medicines can contribute. Get a patch examined if it changes shape or colour, becomes raised or bleeds.

Vitiligo

Vitiligo appears as white patches where the skin has lost its pigment, commonly on the face, hands and around the mouth. It is not contagious and often progresses slowly, and response to any treatment varies widely. Consult early if new patches are appearing, and avoid unverified home remedies.

Dandruff

Dandruff is flaking and itching of the scalp, often worse in winter or during stress. If it comes with noticeable hair thinning, see our page on homeopathy hair fall treatment in Lucknow. Seek a check-up if the scalp is red, oozing or painful.

Urticaria (Hives)

Urticaria causes raised, itchy welts that appear and fade within hours. Triggers include foods, medicines, infections, heat and stress, and sometimes no cause is found. For recurring hives, eczema and related problems, see skin allergy and eczema.

When to seek urgent medical care

Please do not wait for a clinic appointment if you notice any of the following. Go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

  • A rash that is spreading rapidly, or a rash with high fever
  • Blisters over large areas, or sores in the mouth or eyes
  • Swelling of the lips, face or tongue, or difficulty breathing
  • Skin that is hot, very painful or oozing pus
  • A mole or spot that changes quickly, bleeds or does not heal
